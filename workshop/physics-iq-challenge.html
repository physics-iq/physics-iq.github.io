Physics-IQ Challenge @ ICCV 2025

Physics-IQ × Perception Test Challenge

We’re excited to be featured as a guest challenge at the Perception Test Workshop @ ICCV 2025 in Honolulu!

The Physics-IQ Challenge invites you to test the physical reasoning abilities of your image-to-video models—can they distinguish what’s physically plausible?

Eval.ai submission Physics-IQ Home GitHub Perception Test Home tech report (coming soon)

Participants can already explore and evaluate their models using our benchmark by visiting the GitHub page. The Eval.ai server will go live on August 1, 2025.

A short technical report will be required from the top 3 teams and collected in September 2025.
