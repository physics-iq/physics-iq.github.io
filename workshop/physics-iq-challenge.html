Physics-IQ × Perception Test Challenge

We’re excited to be featured as a guest challenge at the Perception Test Workshop @ ICCV 2025 in Honolulu!

The Physics-IQ Challenge invites you to test the physical reasoning abilities of your image-to-video models—can they distinguish what’s physically plausible?
